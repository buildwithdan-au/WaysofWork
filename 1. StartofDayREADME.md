# Next Tasks & Energy Plan

A Glean agent that reads your recent work activity across Xero's tools and tells you **what to work on right now**, plus a **plan for the rest of your day**.

- **Agent:** [Next Tasks and Energy Plan for User](https://app.glean.com/chat/agents/48276e0d1d2f49d29d67c13e62c9fd97)
- **Further documentation:** [Confluence page](https://xero.atlassian.net/wiki/x/2QGuPT8)

---

## What it does

1. **Gathers context** from seven sources (see below).
2. **Prioritises** your open work using the Eisenhower Matrix, suggesting delegates only where it is confident they have the expertise.
3. **Plans your day** with an Energy & Context Switching Plan that puts deep work in your peak-energy hours and batches similar work together.

## Data sources

| Source | What it pulls | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Google Calendar | Upcoming meetings (time constraints, priorities) | Now → +7 days |
| Meetings / transcripts | Past meetings, scanned for action items involving you | Last 30 days |
| Confluence | Pages you edited or viewed | Last 7 days |
| Jira | Three separate searches (below), 30 results each, raw JSON | See below |
| Slack | Messages sent and received, prioritising @mentions | Last 7 days |
| Google Drive | Files you uploaded | Last 7 days |
| Asana | Tasks you created/edited, plus tasks currently assigned to you | Last 7 days / current |

### Jira queries

| # | Query | Date filter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tickets assigned to you | None | Active/open only where supported |
| 2 | Tickets created by you | Past week | Any status |
| 3 | Tickets you watch | Updated in past week | Recent comments, status changes, edits |

The Jira step returns raw search output only. Interpretation happens in the final analysis step.

## Output

**(a) Immediate next tasks**
- Bulleted list, organised by Eisenhower quadrant (Do / Schedule / Delegate / Eliminate)
- Delegation suggestions with a short reason, only when the right person is obvious

**(b) Energy & Context Switching Plan**
- Table for the rest of the day

| Time | Activity | Energy mode |
|---|---|---|
| e.g. 09:00 | Draft strategy memo | Deep work |

Formatting: English, with emojis.

## Usage

1. Open the agent link above in Glean.
2. Run it. No inputs are needed; it works from your own activity.
3. Review the task list and day plan.

Best run at the start of the working day, or after a big meeting block when priorities may have shifted.

## Using it as a Claude skill

`SKILL.md` in this folder reproduces the same workflow for Claude with the Glean connector enabled. Copy the folder into your skills directory (or upload it via Settings → Capabilities → Skills).

## Limitations

- Only sees what you can see: Glean results are permission-filtered.
- "Peak energy hours" are not inferred from data. State yours, or the plan falls back to a sensible default.
- Delegation suggestions are conservative by design and may be empty.

## Maintenance

Edit the agent in Glean, then update this README and `SKILL.md` to match. Keep the Confluence page in sync.
